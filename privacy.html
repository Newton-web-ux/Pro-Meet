This policy explains how Pro Meet (“we”, “us”) handles information when you use the Pro Meet website and meeting service (the “Service”). The Service is operated by [Operator name].

1. In short

  • Audio and video go directly between participants’ browsers. We do not record or store them.
  • We store each room’s chat, shared notes, whiteboard and attendance so the room works and so meeting history can be shown.
  • Anyone who has a room code can read that room’s chat, shared notes and whiteboard. Use long, random room codes for sensitive meetings.
  • Files are only saved to Google Drive when someone clicks Save or Share.
  • Your private notes, drawings and settings stay in your own browser.
  • We do not show ads, sell personal information, or use advertising or analytics trackers.

2. What we collect

What you give us

  • Display name that you type when you join. It is shown to other participants and stored in presence, chat and attendance records.
  • Chat messages, shared notes (with their Note, Action or Decision tag), and whiteboard drawings and text.
  • Files you save or share: whiteboard pages (PNG images), notes (text files) and drawings (PNG images).
  • Meeting titles and schedule that you create on the dashboard.
  • Google account details if you choose to sign in: your name, email address and profile information provided by Google, used to show your name and sync your dashboard.

What is created automatically

  • Anonymous identifier. Joining a meeting creates a random Firebase user ID for that browser tab. It is not linked to your name or email unless you sign in with Google.
  • Presence information: whether your microphone and camera are on, whether your hand is raised or you are sharing your screen, when you joined, and when you were last seen.
  • Attendance records: your display name, join time and last-seen time for each visit to a room.
  • Connection data. To connect directly, browsers exchange network addresses (including IP addresses) and session descriptions through the room. Other participants’ browsers receive them, and the STUN servers we use also see your IP address.
  • Host actions such as mute or remove commands, and host settings such as locked microphones.

What we do not collect

We do not record audio or video, and we do not read your device’s contacts, files or location. Camera, microphone and speaker names are read by your browser to let you choose a device. They are not sent to us.

3. How we use it

  • To run meetings: connect participants, show who is in the room, and deliver chat, notes and the whiteboard.
  • To show meeting history, such as attendance, shared notes and saved files, on your dashboard.
  • To sync your rooms and schedule if you sign in with Google.
  • To keep the Service working and safe, and to fix problems.
  • To follow the law and protect people and the Service.

4. Audio and video

Audio and video go directly between participants using WebRTC and are encrypted in transit. Pro Meet does not route them through our servers. Because the connection is direct, each participant’s browser learns the network address of the others. If you do not want that, do not join the meeting.

Processing such as noise cancellation, volume changes and background effects happens on your device before your audio or video is sent. If you use a background effect, your browser loads the effect’s code from a public content delivery network (jsDelivr). The video itself is not sent there.

6. Who it is shared with

  • Other participants. Everyone in a room can see your name, whether your microphone and camera are on, your chat messages, shared notes and whiteboard drawings, and anything you share to the chat.
  • Google. We use Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore to store data and sign people in, Google Drive and Apps Script to save files, and Google’s public STUN servers to help browsers connect. We also load the Firebase code from Google’s servers. Google processes data under its own terms and privacy policy.
  • Hosting and content delivery. Our pages are served by our hosting provider, and background-effect code is loaded from jsDelivr if you use it. These providers can see your IP address and basic request details when your browser fetches those files.
  • Legal and safety. We may disclose information if the law requires it, or to protect people, the Service or our rights.

We do not sell personal information and we do not share it for advertising.

7. Cookies and local storage

We do not use advertising or analytics cookies. Pro Meet and Firebase use your browser’s storage to work: to keep you signed in, to remember your dashboard, settings and name, and to keep your private notes and drawings. You can clear this at any time in your browser settings. Doing so will remove your private notes, drawings and, for guests, your dashboard.

8. Your choices and rights

  • Leave a meeting to remove your presence record. You can sign out of Google from the dashboard.
  • Delete or edit what you added: you can delete your own shared notes, and any participant can clear a whiteboard page.
  • Choose what to save. Nothing goes to Google Drive unless you click Save or Share.
  • Ask us. You can ask us to give you a copy of your information, correct it, or delete it, by writing to the address in the Contact section. Please include the room code and the approximate time of the meeting so we can find it.

Depending on where you live, you may have more rights under laws such as the GDPR, the UK GDPR, California privacy law or India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, including the right to object, restrict processing, or complain to a regulator. We will respond within the time the law requires.

Some information is shared with other participants or copied by them, so we cannot remove every copy. Attendance records cannot be deleted by participants, but you can ask us to delete them.

9. Security

We use Firebase security rules so that only signed-in users can read or write room data, and so that people can only change their own records. Audio and video are encrypted in transit.

The room code is what protects a room. Chat, notes and whiteboard content are not end-to-end encrypted, and anyone who has the code can read them. Files are stored in the Service operator’s Google Drive. No system is perfectly secure, so please share only what you are comfortable sharing.

10. Children

The Service is not meant for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

11. International transfers

Google and our other providers may process data in countries other than your own. When you join a meeting with people elsewhere, your video, audio and network address are sent to them directly. By using the Service you understand that your information may be processed outside your country.

12. Changes

We may update this policy. The “Last updated” date above shows the latest version. If a change is significant, we will try to tell you on the website.
