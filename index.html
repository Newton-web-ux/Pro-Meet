Stay in charge of the room.

The host is whoever has been in the room longest, and the role passes on automatically when they leave.

  • Mute everyone, stop all video, or lower all raised hands
  • Lock microphones, or allow only the host to share the screen
  • Turn one person's voice level up or down, or remove them

Host controls are applied by each participant's browser. Treat them as meeting etiquette, not as security.

Private by design, honest about the limits.

Here is plainly what stays between participants and what is stored.

Stays between participants

  • Audio and video travel directly between browsers, encrypted in transit by WebRTC. Pro Meet does not record or store them.
  • Private notes and drawings stay in your browser until you choose to save or share them.
  • No ads and no tracking cookies.

Stored for the room

  • Chat, shared notes, whiteboard strokes and attendance are stored in Google Firebase with the room.
  • Anyone with the room code can read them, so use a long, random code for sensitive meetings. They are not end-to-end encrypted.
  • Files you save go to a Google Drive folder owned by whoever runs this Pro Meet service.

Questions, answered.

Do I need an account?

No. Guests join with a name and a room code. Signing in with Google is optional and only syncs your room list, schedule and history across your devices.

How many people can join one room?

Pro Meet connects every participant directly to every other participant, so quality is best with up to about 8 people. Larger rooms can work but use more bandwidth on each device.

Which browsers and devices work?

Current Chrome and Edge give the fullest experience. Firefox and Safari work, but choosing a speaker is not available there and background effects can be slower. Phones and tablets work in their browsers.

Why might someone fail to connect?

Pro Meet uses public STUN servers and no relay server. On very restrictive networks, such as some corporate firewalls, a direct connection may not form. Trying another network or a phone hotspot usually fixes it.

Does Pro Meet record meetings?

No. Audio and video are not recorded or stored. Participants can still capture their own screen, so tell people before you record anything.

Where do saved files go?

When someone clicks Save or Share on the whiteboard or notes, the file goes to a Google Drive folder named after the room, owned by whoever runs this service. A card appears in the chat if it was shared.

How do I find a past meeting?

Open the dashboard. Past meetings list the people who attended, the shared notes written during the meeting and any files saved. Rooms you have joined before are remembered in your browser, or in your account if you signed in.

Are the host controls a security feature?

No. They help run a meeting, but they are applied by each participant's browser. Use a long, random room code if you need to keep uninvited people out.
